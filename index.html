A Screen To A Family Tree

They told me, "Don't fall," keep your heart locked away,
But you hit my phone and changed my whole day.

One little message had me feeling too much,
Trading the distance for a digital touch.

Late-night calls became my ultimate peace,
Watching the walls I built slowly cease.

They said it’s just pixels, it isn't quite real,
But they didn't know how you made my heart feel.

We laughed at the past while building our dreams,
Mending the fabric of life at the seams.

Even when clashing or arguing late,
I'd still choose you, girl, and accept our own fate.

If love is a gamble, I'm betting it all,
Proudly defying the warning to fall.

One day we'll show them the texts that we saved,
All of the late nights and plans that we paved.

They’ll see two young hearts on a glowing screen,
Becoming the greatest thing they've ever seen.

And when our kids ask how the story began,
We’ll say we were teenagers, holding a hand.

From a warning to destiny, bright and so free,
You grew a small screen to a family tree.

You became my forever, my whole reality.
